ESTD. 2023
How We Began

THE DELIVERY-FIRST REVOLUTION

Traditional restaurant models were built for dining rooms, which often meant deliveries were secondary—arriving cold, soggy, and delayed. We saw a need for a fresh start. CRAVEBOX was founded in 2023 with a simple, bold concept: **a kitchen engineered purely for the delivery era.**

By removing the dining room, we focus 100% of our energy, resources, and passion on the food itself and the speed of service. Every burger smash, pizza pull, and chicken fry is optimized to stay fresh, hot, and crispy from our custom cloud kitchen straight to your dining table.
